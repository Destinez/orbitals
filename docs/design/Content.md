FIELD NOTES — SURVEY LOG, ENTRY 1

Every planet with a moon eventually locks part of its system into resonance. Neptune and Pluto complete three orbits to two, a ratio stable enough that Pluto crosses Neptune's path without ever colliding with it. Jupiter's inner moons do something similar. Pierre-Simon Laplace worked out the mechanics in 1784: Io, Europa, and Ganymede fall into a 1:2:4 pattern and hold it, each moon's pull correcting the next.

A resonance is not a coincidence of matching periods. It requires a driver, mass exchanging momentum with mass, one body correcting another every time the ratio starts to slip. Daniel Kirkwood noticed the same principle from the opposite direction in 1866: asteroids at certain distances from the sun are systematically absent, swept clear by resonance with Jupiter. Resonance can announce a body's presence or account for its absence. It does not occur without one.

In 1846, Urbain Le Verrier and John Couch Adams used irregularities in Uranus's orbit to calculate the position of a planet no one had seen. Neptune was found within a degree of the predicted coordinates. The orbit came first. The object came after.

The method is still in use. In 2016, Konstantin Batygin and Mike Brown proposed a ninth planet in the outer solar system, inferred entirely from the clustering of several distant objects' orbits, objects that behave as though something large is shepherding them. The planet has not been directly observed. The clustering has not gone away.

Survey object 0417 was logged the same way. Its orbit carries a 3:2 resonance, clean, stable, consistent with a body of significant mass holding station nearby. The position that mass should occupy was calculated to the same standard used for the 2016 proposal, then checked against every instrument the survey had.

Nothing was there. Signal-to-noise ratio at that coordinate came back below five sigma, the same threshold used to confirm the Higgs boson in 2012 and the same one used to rule out a false positive. Below five sigma, a detection is treated as absent, not merely faint.

The resonance has not decayed in the time since. A resonance without a driver should decay. This one hasn't.

The survey has continued logging every pass. Three thousand two hundred and thirty-two recorded to date. Each one shows the same lock, the same ratio, the same missing mass.

No note has been added speculating on cause. None is warranted yet.
